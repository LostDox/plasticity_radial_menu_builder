## 🏎️ Quick Start
1. **Open the Tool**: Click **[Live Demo](https://LostDox.github.io/plasticity_radial_menu_builder/)** to start building.
2. **Design**: Drag commands from the right-hand list onto the pie chart.
3. **Multi-Menu**: Click the `+` button to create separate menus for Sketching, Modeling, etc.
4. **Export**: 
   - Click **Export** to download a `.radial.json` for Plasticity.
   - Click **Export Full Workspace** to save your entire multi-tab setup as a backup.
5. **Install**: Drag your `.radial.json` into Plasticity. Press `F`, search for your menu name, and right-click to assign a shortcut.

## ✨ QOL Features
### 🌈 Dynamic Color Theming
Every radial menu can have its own identity. Assign one of 14 curated colors, and the entire app—from the background glow to the pie slices—will tint to match. 

### 🗂️ Workspace Backups
Don't start from scratch every time. Export your entire workspace into a single JSON file. Restoring it brings back all your tabs, custom names, and colors instantly.

### 🧩 Multi-Radial Interface
Manage complex workflows with a tabbed interface. Switch between different menus instantly without losing your configuration.

### 🎨 Clean Iconography
Standardized minimalist icon engine. New menus default to a grid icon, and instances use custom isometric cube symbols for a professional look.
